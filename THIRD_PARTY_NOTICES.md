# Third-party artwork

## Concept photos

Concept photos in `public/concepts/` are sourced from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/)
under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The Pexels license
allows free use, including commercial use, and does not require attribution.
The source page for each photo is recorded in `public/concepts/CREDITS.md`.

Two gesture photos are from Wikimedia Commons and are not available under the
